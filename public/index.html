Universal Youth Credential Ledger

What is this?

A shared credential ledger where approved organizations—schools, nonprofits, rec centers, workforce programs—award digital micro-credentials to young people. Teens can store and present these credentials as proof of real skills. Employers and mentors can verify authenticity instantly, without ever calling the issuer.

Issuers Schools, nonprofits & programs that award credentials
Youth Teens who earn and present their verified skills
Verifiers Employers & mentors who check credential authenticity

Credential Ledger

Every credential action—issued, verified, revoked—is recorded here in an immutable, hash-chained log. No edits. No deletions. Full transparency.
