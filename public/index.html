What is MyBlock?

A shared credential ledger where approved organizations (schools, nonprofits, rec centers, workforce programs) award verifiable micro-credentials to youth. Teens build a portable "wallet" of skills. Employers verify instantly with an 8-character code. No crypto, no coins — just a straightforward, tamper-evident record.
